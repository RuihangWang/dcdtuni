The optional `.cfdrun.geometryExport.sourceSurfaces` map binds a source face UID to one export-time ASCII STL solid. `source_component_validation.validate_source_components` verifies the strict V2 manifest and all export-time STL hashes, then derives a planar triangle-support summary for each bound solid. It does not alter any STL, pair two physical skins, declare a fluid gap, or invoke dcCart/OpenFOAM.

Each source summary contains canonical 3D source triangles, a plane and deterministic projection basis, edge-connected components, real triangle footprints, boundary segments, and a winding/order-independent triangle-set digest. Area terms follow the canonical triangle order and use `math.fsum` at both component and source level, so reversing STL facet order does not alter the summary. AABB is only a broad-phase filter; projected triangle overlap is checked by polygon clipping, subject to the unresolved precision case below. A single UID-bound solid with a second parallel layer **beyond the plane tolerance** or overlapping support, nonplanar triangles, duplicate or degenerate triangles, material positive-area overlap, nonmanifold edges or pinched boundary fails. Layers closer than 1e-7 m with disjoint projected support are treated as tolerance-coplanar components; this is not proof they are one physical sheet. Curved and intentionally folded source faces are outside V1. A future `surfaceFace` scope needs its own curved-surface admission contract.

The actual Shinaide `Raised_Floor` source contains valid T-junctions in its planar triangulation. The validator splits edge ownership **in memory** at on-edge source vertices before deriving component boundaries. It never modifies the source STL or expands supported area. The old 284-source and new 354-source packages use the same `merged_floor_ceiling.stl` bytes: SHA-256 `5beca234a9fd8796466560a096a4b3928402ef5c1d4689aed9d4bf7a7e099620`. The complete 354-source export has 74 zero-thickness sheets and 280 surface faces; all 354 bind to one planar component each, covering 752 source triangles. The source manifest SHA-256 and canonical summary digest are in [the evidence JSON](../../benchmarks/shinaide-source-component-validation-2026-09-27.json).

The V1 numeric profile is fixed in the module: plane distance 1e-7 m, triangle area floor 1e-14 m², normal sine 1e-5, positive overlap 1e-12 m², on-edge distance 1e-12 m, and contour-coordinate rounding 1e-10 m with collision refusal above 1e-12 m. These bounds reject ambiguous geometry at their resolution; they do **not** prove geometry below tolerance is physically unique. Contour segments are a source-support summary, not stable engineering contour UIDs. Imported CAD-wide/curved meshes, physical wall-pair admission, partial projection coverage between two solids, faceZone completeness, mesh-stage source support, mesh quality and CFD solving remain separate work.

The in-memory T-junction step currently scans source vertices for each triangle edge, so its worst-case work is O(edges × vertices). A large CAD face needs a spatial index and an explicit performance budget before this can become a production admission gate. The 1e-10 m contour rounding and 1e-12 m on-edge test are conservative read-only V1 precision choices: near points may fail closed; this is **not** a general CAD welding operation. The result carries every 3D and projected source triangle for review and later predicates, which is inexpensive for this 752-triangle Shinaide sample but may require a content-addressed artifact or streaming representation for larger projects.

The validator deliberately remains unconnected to production meshing. [Three review counterexamples](source-component-validation-review-red-2026-09-27.md) remain RED: genuine 5e-13 m² overlap is accepted by the current area threshold, near but noncoincident in-plane edges are welded in the projection graph, and source edges on distinct planes 5e-8 m apart are also welded. Lowering the overlap threshold alone misclassifies valid shared edges of a rotated rectangle and Shinaide `Raised_Floor` as overlap due to floating-point clipping noise. **This candidate must not be merged or used as a production geometry gate until robust predicates resolve these cases.** Targeted non-RED source and adjacent export tests pass; the whole backend test suite has unrelated failures and is not a green acceptance signal for this slice.
