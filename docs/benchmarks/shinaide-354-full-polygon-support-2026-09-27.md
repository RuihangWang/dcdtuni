# Shinaide 354-UID source support at the con_f / Raised_Floor seam

**Isolated CPU diagnostic, 2026-09-27 SGT.** This is a six-face, rank-0 junction check, **not** a whole-mesh or all-354-source-face coverage certificate. No source `.cfdproj` or STL was edited and no wind CFD solve was run. The React-exported `.cfdrun` is SHA-256 `c4e57c8022a29979401a887bcfa228071339e25a750a48cdaa7be6dc4a952dbc`; the mesh used its isolated derivative `691ecb14ad30679e230754b6928d53d84d79e50cd315ca77e39f1c3a5187ae96`, which sets both execution mesh-engine fields to dcCart. The 12 source STL hashes and 354 source-face bindings passed backend validation. Native parser coverage was 74/74 declared internal-baffle regions; parsing an UID does not yet constrain where the mesher puts its faces.

A source-matched `24cba6bf` dcCart build repeated the existing two-sheet native fixture on CPU0–3: the far-from-grid control had 52/52 center samples within source support; the near-grid case had 3 of 12 outside at `02baffles` and exited 2. Its only geometric difference was grid placement. The near-grid case reproduces G1 moving a source coordinate class, then cutting a baffle on the moved sheet; it is a deliberately failing invariant, not a remedy. Full small-case evidence is in `/mnt/lv/home/litianyu/benchmarks/dccart_con_f_source_support_trial_20260927/native-current/`.

The 354-UID derived input was then meshed in an isolated case on 40901 with eight MPI ranks, CPU affinity0–7, nice10, one OpenMP/BLAS thread and CUDA hidden. The existing dcCart source-UID parser left geometry unchanged: its eight partitions' five core `polyMesh` files are **40/40 byte-identical** to the earlier current-source mesh without the UID dictionary. The current `run_mesh_only` initially failed after generating the mesh because its ordinary source-surface receipt assumed serial `constant/polyMesh`; the separately committed partitioned-receipt fix allowed a second identical mesh-only run to finish and verify its receipt. These are different runs and are recorded in [the receipt report](shinaide-partitioned-source-receipt-2026-09-27.md).

For the explicit junction ROI `x=1.485–1.545, y=0.775–0.825, z=2.809–2.825 m`, the source bindings are `con_f → proj_Shinaide/m_22/source-sheet` and `Raised_Floor → proj_Shinaide/m_52/source-sheet`. Their original triangles, not the mesher-welded copies, define the supported footprint. The read-only [polygon probe](shinaide-354-full-polygon-support-2026-09-27.py) projects each selected convex mesh face to its source plane, clips it against every triangle of its explicitly mapped STL solid, and sums nonoverlapping intersection areas. Three synthetic clipping controls passed; source triangles had maximum pairwise overlap `0` for `con_f` and `1.78e-15 m²` for `Raised_Floor`. The [full JSON](shinaide-354-full-polygon-support-2026-09-27.json) retains every sampled face and stage.

At stages `00assemble` and `01carving`, the ROI has no con_f/Floor zone face. **`02baffles` is the first stage with four; all four polygons have source-unsupported area, totalling `2.973733798659062e-4 m²`.** The same four polygons and area persist through `03splitwall`, `04relabel`, `05seamsplit`, `08preghost`, and `09dropghost`. In the final rank-0 `polyMesh`, the three affected cells (`46965–46967`) carry six explicitly mapped boundary faces. **All six** have some unsupported area, totalling `2.55861255848977e-4 m²`. Two `Raised_Floor` faces whose *centroids* passed the earlier diagnostic each still have `5.566377743e-6 m²` (about 5.59%) outside the source triangles. Thus the previous center-only result “4 of 6 unsupported” was incomplete; it did not establish that either of the other two polygons was fully supported. Stage and final totals sample different face sets and must not be interpreted as a conserved area difference.

The input `con_f` wall plane is at x≈`1.490737 m` with bottom z≈`2.814873 m`; the nearby source floor contour has x≈`1.490738 m` and a local z edge≈`2.815872 m`. Native G1 logs move the x class near `1.49074 → 1.49307 m` and z classes near `2.81587/2.81687 → 2.81248 m` before `cutBaffles`. These source coordinates differ by about 1 µm in x and 0.999 mm in z. The correlation with the unsupported polygons is strong, but the recorded intent of that junction is unknown: do not invent a contact, physical wall pair, bridge area, or adjusted hole merely from nearness. A valid fix must retain immutable source support through G1 and cut, or reject a project that cannot meet both support and mesh quality until the geometry/contact is authored explicitly.

The new run's authoritative `checkMesh -allTopology -allGeometry -parallel` still reports **six failed checks**: 17 high-aspect cells, 294 bad face tets, 76 low-determinant cells, 34,727 concave cells, 295 low interpolation-weight faces and 609 low volume-ratio faces. The receipt fix changes no geometry. No CFD solve, NV calibration or wind/liquid prediction follows from these results.

The polygon probe is intentionally narrow: it requires the explicit Shinaide `con_f`/`Raised_Floor` mapping and convex selected faces, checks projected support area rather than normal-offset admissibility, and makes no assertion about other assets, ranks or all source UIDs.
