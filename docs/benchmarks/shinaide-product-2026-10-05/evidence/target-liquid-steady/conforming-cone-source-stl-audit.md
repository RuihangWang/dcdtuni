# Source STL and first carving-stage diagnosis

This is a read-only diagnosis. It changes no STL, physical geometry, native source, binary, mesh coordinates or quality threshold, and performs no native/API/solver execution. It does not authorize a user case.

The retained original exported merged_rack_frame.stl and merged_server_9U.stl match the exact SHA-256 identities in the failed eight-rank native preflight matrix. Sixteen physical rejected base faces bind to their corresponding STL solid names. The two internal mesh faces have no source-patch identity and are explicitly omitted from source-facet attribution. Every physical-face vertex lies within the configured carve ON band of a triangle of its own raw source patch; the largest point-to-triangle distance is approximately 0.289948 mm.

The original log reports level 3 and finest spacing approximately 0.0316667 m. From the actual background breakpoints and the default onToleranceCarveFraction=0.01, the configured carve ON band is 0.0003166666662500006 m. Raw exported STL coordinates are distinguished from the native processed surfaces: the old log also records surface snaps and an axis-plane weld. This audit does not substitute the raw ASCII triangles for the processed native cutting planes.

The three Rack_2_frame five-point faces straddle a source corner. For face 143243, its last point is within 7.15255771277512e-11 m of the actual horizontal STL triangle 28 at y=0.04445. The same point is about 0.296620 mm from the outer inclined wall plane x+z=9.780300. Three other points are in the inclined-wall portion and differ from that facet by approximately 3.9395, 48.9262 and 52.8457 micrometres. Thus proximity to the whole shell does not mean that every cap vertex lies on the particular facet carrying the cap. The upper-y faces show the analogous raw-facet distinction, but their first construction stages are not covered by the retained debug box.

The current source observations explain how such a cap can survive. Shell point classification at line 3904 uses nearest distance to the entire shell. The crossing callback skips a real hit within 4*tol of an ON endpoint and can return -2, retaining that endpoint. The cap routine uses a Newell normal and an arithmetic vertex centre; its planarity gate at line 623 is max(0.001*sqrt(area), onTol). The reconstructed final pentagons have about 84.825 micrometres deviation under this particular definition, below the 316.667 micrometre cap gate. The stricter face diagnostic based on the OpenFOAM face centre records about 78.53 micrometres; these two centre definitions must not be mixed. A one-region cap considered planar can return its original loop. Feature-plane folded splitting also requires an explicit DCCART_FOLDSPLIT option. These are source observations, not a recorded branch trace.

The precision-17 native stage dump gives an actual stage boundary for face 143243. Its complete five-point float64 coordinate multiset is absent from 00assemble and present exactly in 01carving.3.txt, line 78, on boundary patch 17 with owner key 3:479387088584788:16898221365475658310. It persists with the same coordinate bits through every later recorded stage to 16skewrepair. The native cell key changes after rekeying; it is not used as geometry identity. The dump sorts face points and does not retain their cyclic winding, so this audit claims coordinate-multiset identity only. The nonplanarity already exists at the carving checkpoint; coalesce/merge/skew are not its first source. The checkpoint includes feature pre-splits, shell clipping and cap processing, so it does not establish which internal substep first introduced the deviation. The y=0.03..0.045 debug box excludes faces 143244 and 143247, whose first stages remain unobserved.

A separate mathematical check preserves two raw rank witness files for the new Liquid internal bases 2084724, 2036954 and 2036955. Their reported deviation is approximately 9.664e-11 m. Exhaustively enumerating the six canonical original-vertex fan anchors finds no nonzero fan satisfying the existing 1e-10 area-and-centre guard. Among fans retaining nonzero areas and unsigned area, the best relative centre errors are 1.3647980555808357e-10, 2.5209308711939404e-10 and 2.5209947081384435e-10. Area vectors alone can agree at roundoff while the centre moment changes. This check rules out only that proposed original-vertex-fan bypass; it does not rule out every possible subdivision.

The independently observed seam source code stores actual candidate coordinates but formerly reconstructs a cutting plane from its rounded 1 nm grouping key. In the Liquid witness this can mix y=0.8999999761581421 with y=0.899999976. Air is separately correcting that source mechanism by transporting actual doubles across all ranks, refusing distinct real coordinates sharing a key and retaining all descendant/zone/share/clean-plane guards. No such C++ change is implemented by this audit.

Reproduction tools are audit_air_cone_source_stl.py, audit_air_cone_carving_stages.py and audit_air_cone_warped_fans.py. Their actual outputs, source hashes, original STL/log/dictionary bytes, all 15 rank-3 stage files, native run receipts and raw internal witnesses are preserved alongside this document. All three pure tools returned zero. The retained-source hash equality, exact first-stage identity, complete later-stage persistence and failed fan guard assertions were checked against the raw JSON.

A sound future correction must construct globally consistent intersections and facet-conforming caps from the actual source planes, preserving all incident cell topology and coupled consistency. Moving one shared final vertex onto a selected plane or relaxing checkMesh cannot be justified by this evidence.
