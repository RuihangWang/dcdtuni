# Independent serial roof component witness audit

The native serial mesh diagnostic uses the same physical Tri scene through React/API, with one meshing process to remove unknown remote MPI neighbour centres. This is not a serial CFD solver run. Full mesh checking still rejects the written mesh, and no roof displacement is written. All IDs in the preserved component JSONL belong to the pre-write cutMesh, not the final polyMesh.

The original three samples are incomplete cell exports. Their original bytes have SHA256 3a60e1192e840e0462213092744e92e74a477b4aa04ba389ecd6a3ea9ce78bc2. The source log reports three components: a 1414-packet/10079-cell star with 51 conflicting points; a 6-packet/60-cell star rejected for planarity; and a 27-packet/280-cell star rejected by the strict cell-face-plane gate. These complete native totals are source observations, not inferred by summing the small samples.

The six-packet sample exports all displacement proposals, although only 32 of its 60 star cells are present. The proposals are consistent and the 24 moving points have no protection bits. The original 32 cells have no nonplanar faces. Applying the complete six-packet displacement to these complete local cell polygons produces 21 distinct nonplanar faces in 18 of the exported cells; maximum deviation is 31.25 micrometres. This is sufficient to demonstrate a local failure. It does not claim the statistics of the missing 28 cells or a complete component-quality result.

A minimal actual witness is native cell 172, face 1417, with ordered point IDs [8439, 300, 9000, 8440]. All original Y coordinates are 0.5 m. Only point 9000 moves, by (0, -0.0000625, 0) m. The four trial points have scalar triple product -1.0094179307554954e-6 cubic metres, so they cannot lie in one plane. The installed OF face-area/centroid representation gives a 15.624999 micrometre face deviation, versus the native cell-span tolerance 4.378807635564444e-11 m. This geometric failure follows even though adjacent roof proposals agree at shared points.

The initial 27-packet sample exports only eight packets. It is not used for a partial displacement trial: omitted packets could move visible points and invalidate such a trial. One source-supported lead is native cell 18834 and its faces 69273/69274 on X=3.94020968925, away from the sole sampled proposal plane X=4.0035430225. Their outward normal is (-1, 0, 0), and their distinct OF face centres have zero normal dot product. Thus adaptive coplanar fragments elsewhere in the full point star can still obstruct the strict gate. This initial lead is distinct from the subsequently exported actual first trigger below.

The independent tool permits local evaluation from a complete packet proposal list or an explicit complete-component final delta for every exported point. It refuses a missing delta, any nonfinite delta, disagreement with visible packet proposals, or any native-reported global conflict, even when the conflicting point is omitted from the sample. Eleven focused tests pass, including all three actual version-two trigger records. No source C++, binary, native mesh, solver, checker threshold or source STL is changed by this audit.


The version-two JSONL adds the actual trigger IDs and complete-component final delta for every exported point. Its original bytes have SHA256 9769ceef7e5f652dd6df61c48958b6fce1763442fdc8761b83286a99034143e3. The initial raw is retained separately. Exact source records reproduce all three native rejection reasons:

* Conflict: unprotected point 21946 receives (0.00003166666675, 0, 0) m from cell 3699 and (0, -0.00003125, 0) m from cell 3990. Their axes/planes and vectors are incompatible. One conflict is sufficient to reject the component; the export does not independently reproduce all 51 reported conflicts.
* Planarity: the native trigger is cell 172 / face 1417 / point 8439. Its calculated normal offset is 1.5624999055062486e-5 m, greater than the 4.378807635564444e-11 m relative tolerance. Point 8439 itself is stationary; moving another corner changes the face plane.
* Strict cell-plane: the actual first trigger is cell 6378, outside the moving-point star but included in the one-ring quality closure, with faces 22890 and 22891. All seven distinct points of those faces have complete-component final delta (0, 0, 0). The original and trial OF centres, normals and cell volume are identical. Both outward normals are (-1, 0, 0), and the normalized face-to-other-centre dot product is exactly zero, greater than the native limit -1.0001e-6. The cell is vertex-convex with planar polygons and coplanar fragments. This reproduces the exact native rejection without inferring omitted packet displacements.

A native reason6 result does not imply every cell in the component passed planarity, closure or positive volume. The implementation checks these gates within each cell and stops at the first failure. The early one-ring cell6378 fails its strict face-centre gate before later cells are examined. Applying the recorded complete-component final deltas to the 32 exported cells exposes 31 distinct nonplanar faces in 23 cells, with maximum deviation 9.732065084947629e-6 m. This independently disproves a claim of full-star planarity success. Counts remain local to the partial export, and no complete-component or user-case quality acceptance is inferred.
