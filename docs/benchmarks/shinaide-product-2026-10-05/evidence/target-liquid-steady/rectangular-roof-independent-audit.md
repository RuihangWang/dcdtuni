# Independent rectangular buffered-roof diagnostic

The exact54-cell roof fixture admits a conservative affine rectangular extension. This is a mathematics and native-checker diagnostic; it does not establish that any original Tri or Shinaide Liquid production packet meets fine-neighbour, protected-point or full-star conditions.

Dimensions are read from the saved binary-mesh preflights: Tri rank0 cell11 has widths(0.253333334,0.25,0.255010846)m; Liquid rank0 cell29 has widths(0.25,0.255555556,0.246875)m. The latter has a four-face transition group on its negativeX side, but its fine-neighbour qualification is explicitly still unproved by that preflight.

The prototype applies the same original normalized roof to the exact54-cell interior buffer and then a positive-determinant affine transformation. For each dimension set, all three normal axes and both signs pass the pure metrics: closed directed edge cycles, face planarity, convex face support, vertex halfspace support, the installed strict face-centre criterion, positive original-point/centre tet fans, internal/coupled area determinant, interpolation weight and adjacent volume ratio. All12 actual-size variants pass. Six aspect1.1 boundary samples also pass.

For an affine matrixA, oriented face areas transform asSf'=det(A)A^-T Sf. This preserves area closure, plane incidence and vertex halfspace signs. All fixture faces are planar convex polygons, so their fan-area weights share one per-face factor and their centroids transform asfc'=Afc. Signed pyramid cell volume and centroid then transform asV'=det(A)V andcc'=Acc. Face-normal owner/neighbour distance integrals both gain det(A); weights and volume ratios are invariant. Signed tet volume gains det(A)>0, so orientation remains positive. Circumradius-normalized quality still requires evaluation.

Letkappa be the ratio of the largest to smallest singular value. The inward normalized face-centre dot retains at least1/kappa of its original magnitude. For the area tensorT=Σouter(Sf/mean|Sf|), det(T')/det(T)≥kappa^-6. The unit fixture's minimum inward dot is0.000499999875 and minimum determinant is0.124999890555. A deliberately narrow max/min width ratio≤1.1 therefore retains an inward dot≥0.000454545341 and determinant≥0.070559179478. Both exceed the installed strict thresholds; this algebra does not waive any other quality check.

A useful recognition scope is finite extents>1micrometre, max/min≤1.1, one axis-aligned rectangular coarse box and exactly four uncut half-extents boxes, the same normalized3×3 interface grid, and displacementη=0.0005 of the normal extent. Every original protection, overlap and complete point-star requirement remains mandatory. This is bounded rectangular support, not arbitrary aspect ratios, arbitrary clipping or a proof about original-mesh candidate eligibility.

One actual2-rank checker diagnostic uses the Liquid dimensions with negativeX roof,54 cells,130 points,222 faces and129 internal faces. A fixed18/36 coarse/fine split produces genuine cross-rank faces. OpenFOAM2412 checkMesh -constant -allTopology -allGeometry -parallel reports Mesh OK. Its minimum determinant is0.124923, minimum weight0.333056, minimum volume ratio0.124906 and maximum nonorthogonality25.3116°. Face tets, concavity and flatness pass. Source coordinates, face/owner/neighbour arrays, decomposition, dictionaries, stdout and hashes are preserved.

Seven focused tests pass. The first environment setup lacked OpenFOAM activation and did not spawn decomposePar; that failure log is retained. The successful checker ran once after correct conda activation. No production solver, mesher, C++ source, executable or original STL was changed. Full user-case acceptance remains pending.
