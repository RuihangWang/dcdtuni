ROOT CAUSE of the convergence gap (bandAbsorb cannot reach it):
  The k-collapse driver is a thin WALL wedge at min wall distance y_P=1.30e-4 m
  (healthy meshes floor at 5.0e-4; epsilonWallFunction eps=2*k*nu/y^2 blows up
  below it). That wedge is a bandAbsorb candidate (t<h/8) but its union with
  EVERY neighbour is concave -> gate G3 foamOut refuses it (a concave cell would
  fail checkMesh's face-pyramid check, so the gate is mandatory). It is too
  voluminous to kill (V/Vref 9.6e-8..2.6e-3 vs killVolumeFraction 1e-6 path) and
  repairThinWallCells (minWallCellDistance 4e-4) is OFF by default and documented
  "not a win" on DAML (75 stranded, max skew 3.97->4.81, Mesh OK->Failed;
  dcCartCutCells.H:162-179). bandAbsorb removed the tiniest faces (min face
  5.84e-9 -> 5.81e-8) and 214 thin plates, but the specific y_P killer is
  concave-unabsorbable. Curing it needs an upstream fix (PTWELD-style welding of
  the near-coincident/tilted junction that creates the wedge, or surface snapping),
  which is outside the S9e merge mechanism and outside the allowed file scope.
